What I'd Tell a Friend Building Their Support Stack in Immigration Practice

Immigration attorneys get better results designing case management software and paralegal support as one system rather than choosing between them. Software carries date-driven, repeatable work: docketing, form population, exhibit indexing, receipt logging. Paralegals carry interpretation: record review, client follow-up, adapting to new guidance, escalation judgment. Eligibility calls, strategy, and the signature stay with the attorney. Flexibility comes from blending fixed and on-demand capacity and piloting one case type first.
