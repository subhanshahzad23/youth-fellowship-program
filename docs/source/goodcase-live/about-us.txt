FAQs
A few things you may want to ask us
Where are you based?

Our company is based in Europe (Poland). Our team is based in the United States, Europe, South America, and Asia serving clients globally.

Are you a law firm?

No, we’re not a law firm. We are a team of business consultants, paralegals and legal writers who assist immigration attorneys in scaling their business.

What makes your services different from a standard virtual assistant agency?

Unlike generalist VA agencies that handle basic administrative tasks, we are specialized U.S. immigration paralegals and business consultants. Our team has supported over 1,000 cases, ranging from complex family-based petitions to high-stakes employment visas. We don’t just "do tasks", we understand the nuances of USCIS requirements, the urgency of priority dates, and the specific workflow of an immigration law firm.

How can I reach out to you?

By email (info@goodcase.io) or using the contact form below. We reply to all emails within 24 hours.
