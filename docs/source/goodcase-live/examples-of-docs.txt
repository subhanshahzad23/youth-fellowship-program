Exhibits Format for Form I-129 / O-1A Visa

For an O-1A visa, the Form I-129 is the core legal document, but it is the Exhibit Package that actually proves your case. USCIS officers typically spend only a few minutes reviewing a petition initially, so the structure of your exhibits must be logical, easy to navigate, and clearly mapped to the legal criteria.

Sample EB-2 NIW Business Plan

An EB-2 NIW business plan acts as a roadmap for USCIS, demonstrating the applicant's ability to advance their proposed project, which must serve national importance. It is a detailed document proving they qualify for a green card without a job offer.

Preview
Letter of Recommendation for 0-1A Visa

A letter of recommendation is a formal testimonial written by a recognized expert in your field. Its primary purpose is to provide context and validation for your achievements. These letters translate your technical accomplishments into a narrative that proves you are "at the very top of your field."

Preview
Sample O-1 Petition Brief

While your exhibits provide the proof, the legal brief provides the argument in your O-1 package. It is narrative drafted by your petitioner (employer/agent) or attorney that explains to a USCIS officer exactly why you meet the legal definition of "extraordinary."
